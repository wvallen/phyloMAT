About phyloMAT
phyloMAT is a phylogenomics interface for proteome-scale questions: where motifs/domains concentrate, where they split, and where they appear independently across deep time.

This page is an interactive explorer shell. Motif search + exported results will be connected as a separate, verifiable analysis engine later.
