phyloMAT

phyloMAT is a causal taxonomy atlas. It represents selected biological lineages and transitions as a topological structure organized by constraint, adjacency, and shared structure.

Time is implicit. Uncertainty is represented spatially. Coverage expands as additional regions are explored.

Instrument

The atlas is interactive and dynamic. Elements briefly adjust when layers or regions change, then settle into a stable configuration.

The structure supports layered overlays, including protein motifs, domains, and other recurring biological patterns.

FAQ

What does this map contain?
The atlas contains taxa and transitions that have been examined so far. It is extended through manual iteration.

What does motif analysis mean here?
Motifs include protein domains, regulatory structures, cellular capabilities, and recurring biological strategies.

How is uncertainty handled?
Uncertainty is represented using fog and confidence values.

How is the atlas updated?
The atlas exports local context and a structured prompt. A language model proposes an XML patch, which is reviewed and incorporated manually.
